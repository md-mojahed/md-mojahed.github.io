Why this works (a little)

The glowing path rises for 4 seconds and falls for 6. That's about 6 breaths a minute with a longer exhale.

  • Slow breathing: breathing near 6 breaths per minute is linked to higher heart-rate variability, a marker of a calmer nervous system (Lehrer & Gevirtz, 2014).
  • Longer exhales: in a 2023 study, a few minutes a day of exhale-focused breathing improved mood more than mindfulness meditation did (Balban et al., Cell Reports Medicine).
  • Naming worries: putting a label on a stressful thought, then watching it pass, is a core move in Acceptance & Commitment Therapy. That's what the clouds are for.
  • No fail state: no lives, no game over, every note in harmony. Your brain gets novelty without threat.

It's a toy, not therapy. If stress is heavy and constant, talk to someone you trust or a professional. 💙
